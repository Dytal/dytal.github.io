PRIVACY, IN PLAIN LANGUAGE

Your data, your flight.

This notice explains what Sky Hopper does in the browser, what optional cloud accounts store, and how third-party services may be involved. It is a project privacy notice, not legal advice.

Important before launch: the site owner should review this notice against the deployed configuration, publish a monitored contact method, and configure the consent options required by applicable law and Google’s publisher policies. Do not claim that a service is inactive if it is enabled in the deployed site.

1. Playing as a guest

You can play the core game without registering. During a guest session, score, coins, XP, flight history, achievements, relic counts and upgrades stay in page memory and disappear when the page is refreshed or closed. They are not sent to Supabase as guest progression.

If you set a guest pilot name, the site stores that display-name preference in this browser’s local storage so it can be shown again on this device. This preference is separate from game progression. Guest pilot names are not submitted to the global leaderboard. Browser settings or clearing site data can remove the saved name.

2. Optional accounts and cloud progression

If the site owner has configured Supabase, you may register with an email address, password and pilot name. Supabase Auth processes credentials; Sky Hopper does not read or store your password directly. Signed-in profiles can store a pilot name, best score, XP and level. Related tables store flight totals, Arcade points, coins, flight records, achievement codes, relic inventory, upgrades, purchased cosmetics and equipped appearance.

The shared leaderboard can display your pilot name, best score and level. Your email address is not intentionally rendered on public leaderboard rows. Do not use a pilot name that reveals your real name, school, address, phone number or another identifying detail. You can change your pilot name inside the game; an authenticated name may take a short time to appear everywhere while the leaderboard refreshes.

Authentication sessions are configured to remain in page memory rather than local storage. This means you may need to sign in again after a refresh even though cloud progression remains in the database.

3. Google AdSense and advertising

The site source includes the Google AdSense publisher script. Whether an ad is actually served depends on the publisher account, site approval, ad configuration and Google’s systems. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, local storage, device signals or similar technologies to provide, measure, limit or personalise ads, subject to the choices available and the applicable policies. Google may receive normal network data, including an IP address, when your browser requests its services.

For current details, read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google’s Privacy Policy. The publisher must configure an appropriate consent message and ad-personalisation options in AdSense Privacy & messaging. For personalised ads in the UK, EEA or Switzerland, Google requires a Google-certified consent management platform integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework.

4. Other third-party services

The game loads the Supabase JavaScript library from jsDelivr and, when configured, communicates with the project’s Supabase database. The main stylesheet requests fonts from Google Fonts. These services are operated by third parties and may receive network request information needed to deliver their resources. Review the relevant providers’ current privacy documentation for their practices.

5. Analytics

The included project code does not add a separate analytics product such as Google Analytics. If the site owner adds one later, this notice and the applicable consent configuration must be updated before the new integration is enabled.

6. Children and young visitors

Do not submit sensitive information or use a public pilot name that identifies you offline. The site owner should review applicable age, privacy and account requirements before inviting children or young teens to create accounts. The site does not ask for date of birth, phone number or exact location as part of normal gameplay.

7. Retention, access and account deletion

Guest gameplay progress is temporary. A chosen guest name remains in browser storage until it is changed or site data is cleared. Signed-in progression remains in the configured Supabase database until it is deleted by the account owner or site operator under the project’s deletion process. Because account deletion requires coordinated removal of the Auth user and related rows, the owner should provide a monitored private contact channel and verify the database cascade before launch.

Never post your email address, account details or other personal information in a public bug report. For general game issues, use the Contact page. A private deletion/request channel must be maintained by the owner.

8. Changes to this notice

This notice may change when the game or its third-party integrations change. The date below indicates the most recent editorial review of this page; check this page again after significant updates.
