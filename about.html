About Sky Hopper.

Sky Hopper is a tiny browser arcade game built around a timeless idea: tap, fly through the gap, and give it one more try. Its original vector-style bird, gates, clouds and landscapes are drawn in the browser with Canvas and CSS.

Two ways to fly

Classic keeps the challenge simple: one collision ends a run. Arcade starts at Normal and ramps through Swift, Hard, Expert and Chaos. It adds health, three skills and 120 collectible relic designs. Both modes are free, and the workshop uses gameplay-earned coins only.

Guest play first

You can play without creating an account, but guest progress exists in memory only and disappears when you refresh or close the page. Once Supabase is configured, signed-in players can sync their scores, XP, Arcade-earned coins, flight history, relic inventory, purchased cosmetics, equipped looks, upgrades and 1,000 achievement milestones to the cloud. No game progress is saved to browser storage.

A small promise

Sky Hopper is meant to be a fun personal project, not a pay-to-win game. The Flight Workshop sells upgrades for coins earned by playing: 1 coin for each 100 cumulative Arcade points, with prices capped at 100 coins. There are no real-money purchases or paid ability packs.
