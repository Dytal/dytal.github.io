Sky Hopper FAQ

Answers about playing, account options and the way the game’s reward systems work. If your question is not covered, use the contact page to report a reproducible issue.

Playing the game

How do I flap?

Press Space, Arrow Up or W, click the game area, or tap it on a touch screen. Each input gives the bird a short upward impulse. Press P or Escape to pause and resume an active flight.

Which mode should I start with?

Choose Classic if you want to learn the basic movement without abilities or pickups. Choose Arcade for multiple hearts, relics, three active skills and increasing difficulty tiers.

Why does one collision end my Classic run?

That is the intended Classic rule. Arcade uses a heart-based system, while Classic is designed as a direct timing challenge.

Why are there so many achievements?

The achievement atlas groups 1,000 milestones into score, flight, relic collection, rare finds, Arcade score and store-purchase tracks. Early milestones are reachable quickly; the highest tiers are intentionally long-term challenges.

Pilot names and accounts

Can I set a custom name without registering?

Yes. Choose a name in the Pilot profile panel. A guest name is saved in browser storage on that device and can be changed later. This setting does not save your scores or coins, and guest names are not sent to the global leaderboard.

How do I change the name on my cloud account?

Sign in, open the Pilot profile panel and choose Change pilot name. Names use 3–16 letters, numbers or underscores and are case-insensitive. A name must be unique; if it is already taken, choose another.

Why does my guest progress disappear?

Guest progression is temporary by design. Refreshing or closing the page resets guest scores, coins, XP, relic counts, awards and session history. Sign in to sync progress to the configured Supabase database.

Why do I need to sign in again after a refresh?

The current front-end configuration keeps authentication sessions in page memory instead of browser storage. You may therefore need to sign in again; your cloud profile and progression remain in Supabase.

Relics, rewards and the store

Why did a relic appear close to a pipe?

Relics move through the same world as the gates. The current build places them beyond a horizontal clearance zone to reduce visual overlap. If an overlap still happens, send the build version, browser/device and the steps that caused it via the bug-report link.

Why did I get zero coins after an Arcade run?

Coins are credited once your cumulative Arcade score crosses a new 100-point milestone. A run can add points without reaching the next full threshold. Classic runs never award coins.

Can I buy coins with real money?

No. The project has no real-money checkout. Coins come from Arcade score milestones and are used for in-game cosmetics and upgrades.

Does a cosmetic improve my bird’s hitbox?

Cosmetics are intended to change appearance only. Permanent upgrades affect the ability, collection or score systems described in the Bazaar; the store displays the listed effect before purchase.

Technical support

The page is slow or the canvas is blank. What should I try?

First reload the page and use a current browser version. Close other heavy tabs, check browser console errors if you know how, and temporarily disable extensions that block scripts. If the problem continues, include your browser/version, device, mode and exact reproduction steps in a report.

How do I report a bug or ask for a feature?

Use the Contact page to open the project’s issue tracker. Please do not put account passwords, email confirmation links or other private information in a public issue.
